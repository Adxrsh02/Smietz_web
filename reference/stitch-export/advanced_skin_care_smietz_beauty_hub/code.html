Frequently Asked Questions

Most of our advanced aesthetic procedures, such as Hydra Facial, RF Tightening, and Microdermabrasion, have zero required recovery time. You can seamlessly resume work or social commitments right away. For micro needling, a gentle pink flush typically settles within a few hours.
Your comfort is central to our philosophy. Our clients generally describe treatments as soothing, cooling, or mildly warming. For protocols that stimulate the skin, like micro needling, we apply a gentle calming balm beforehand to ensure your experience is relaxing.
Skin condition is ever-changing due to climate, stress, and lifestyle. A private skin evaluation allows Sushama and our senior team to assess skin hydration, barrier integrity, and sensitivity before recommending any targeted aesthetic therapy.
We recommend pausing active exfoliants (like glycolic acid or retinol) 48 to 72 hours prior to your session, staying well hydrated, and avoiding direct prolonged sun exposure. We will furnish specific post-care guidance suited to your skin during your appointment.
