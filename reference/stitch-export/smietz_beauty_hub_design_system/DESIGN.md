## Brand & Style

This design system defines an elevated, modern luxury aesthetic tailored for high-end beauty, clinical skincare, and hair styling in Mumbai's urban milieu. It merges classic editorial refinement with tactile, contemporary clarity—stripping away visual clutter in favor of generous whitespace, luminous surfaces, and precise craftsmanship.

### Aesthetics & Tone
- **Sensory Luxury & Warmth:** Soft ivory backgrounds replace stark sterile clinical whites, creating an environment that feels restorative, indulgent, and intimate.
- **Editorial Poise:** High-contrast serif headlines paired with airy, modern grotesk numerals and body copy project poise, heritage, and clinical competence.
- **Controlled Opulence:** Metallic gold and botanical emerald green serve as architectural flourishes rather than loud washes, balancing bespoke salon warmth with high-performance dermatological authority.

## Colors

The color palette is calibrated for soft, radiant contrast, evoking natural botanical oils, ivory stone, and polished brass fixtures.

### Roles & Application
- **Base Canvas (`#FCF9F2`):** Warm ivory foundation used for viewport backgrounds, sections, and atmospheric layers.
- **Surface Elevation (`#FFFFFF`):** Crisp pure white applied to interactive cards, popovers, and elevated containers to induce clean contrast against the ivory base.
- **Primary Brand Accent (`#006D56`):** Deep emerald green used for primary brand touchpoints, badges, secondary actions, and high-impact navigational items.
- **Metallic Accent (`#C9A45C`):** Warm metallic gold dedicated strictly to hairline borders, section dividers, decorative flourishes, star ratings, and tracked section eyebrows.
- **Conversion / CTA Accent (`#F47721`):** Vibrant saffron orange reserved exclusively for high-intent booking paths, instant consultation triggers, and WhatsApp conversion points.
- **Typography & Structural Lines:** Text primary uses `#1A2621` (deep forest slate) for high contrast without the harshness of pure black; secondary text uses `#55635C`; borders use `#E8E2D2` or translucent gold `rgba(201, 164, 92, 0.25)`.

## Typography

The typographic system creates an interplay between the graceful, sculptural presence of **Playfair Display** and the clean, geometric clarity of **Plus Jakarta Sans**.

### Typographic Hierarchy Rules
- **Eyebrows & Badges:** Always set in `eyebrow` uppercase with wide tracking (`0.18em`), rendered in `#006D56` or `#C9A45C` to introduce section themes with quiet discipline.
- **Headlines:** Set with natural sentence case or title case. Large display sizes require negative tracking to tighten the elegant serif proportions.
- **Body Text:** Never drop below `16px` for standard narrative reading on desktop and mobile. Line heights are intentionally generous (`1.5` to `1.6`) to provide an airy, luxurious cadence.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a responsive 12-column grid system anchored by wide side margins that mirror high-end fashion and lifestyle periodicals.

### Breakpoints & Structure
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout, `1.25rem` outer margins, `1rem` column gutters. Single-column stacked cards and horizontal-scroll snap carousels for treatment offerings.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout, `2rem` outer margins, `1.5rem` gutters. Two-column grid configurations for service menus and team highlights.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout max-width `1280px` centered, `3.5rem` outer margins, `2rem` gutters. Asymmetrical editorial compositions and alternating media-text showcases.

### Rhythmic Philosophy
Section spacing relies on comfortable vertical breathing room (`space-xl` scaled dynamically up to `5rem` between distinct salon rituals), avoiding dense utility packaging.

## Elevation & Depth

Rather than relying on deep, muddy drop shadows, this design system achieves dimension using tonal surface layering, frosted translucence, and whisper-light golden reflections.

### Surface Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Foundation):** `#FCF9F2` canvas surface. Grounded, warm, and natural.
- **Tier 1 (Surface Containers):** Crisp `#FFFFFF` containers bounded by `1px solid #E8E2D2` or `1px solid rgba(201, 164, 92, 0.25)`.
- **Tier 2 (Floating & Interactive):** Card hover states lift with a subtle upward translation (`-3px`) paired with a luminous ambient shadow: `0 12px 28px -6px rgba(26, 38, 33, 0.06), 0 0 0 1px rgba(201, 164, 92, 0.35)`.
- **Tier 3 (Sticky Nav & Overlays):** Sticky headers employ frosted glassmorphism: background of `rgba(252, 249, 242, 0.82)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(14px)` and a hairline border bottom `1px solid rgba(201, 164, 92, 0.2)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs **Pill-shaped (Level 3)** geometry for interactive touch targets and refined, softer curves for containers.

### Form Factors
- **Pill Targets (Radius `9999px`):** Used universally across primary buttons, WhatsApp triggers, status chips, and pricing tags to provide organic, tactile softness.
- **Cards & Visual Containers (`rounded-xl` / `1.5rem`):** Editorial service tiles, treatment bundles, and testimonial blocks use large curved corners to avoid rigid corporate sharpness.
- **Framed Imagery:** Masonry gallery photographs and editorial portraits feature softened corners (`1rem` to `1.5rem`) occasionally paired with inset hairline borders for a framed, fine-art feel.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary CTA (Orange High-Intent):** Full pill (`border-radius: 9999px`), background `#F47721`, text `#FFFFFF`, font weight 600. Includes a micro-lift hover state (`translateY(-2px)`) with shadow `0 8px 20px -4px rgba(244, 119, 33, 0.35)`.
- **WhatsApp Direct Trigger:** Pill button in signature `#006D56` or `#F47721`, integrated brand WhatsApp vector icon aligned to the left of label text. Sticky bottom-right floating variant features a subtle continuous pulse ripple in muted gold.
- **Secondary / Ghost Button:** Transparent background, `1.5px solid #006D56`, text `#006D56`, filling to emerald green on hover with crisp white text.

### Service Cards & Editorial Showcase
- **Aspect Ratio & Framing:** Vertical ratio `4:5` or `3:4` for treatment cards. Pure white card body, hairline border `1px solid #E8E2D2`.
- **Content Hierarchy:** Eyebrow duration/category tag in gold, serif card title in `#1A2621`, concise treatment overview, followed by the price indicator and an inline arrow booking trigger.
- **Hover Transitions:** Card moves `-4px` along the Y-axis, hairline border shifts smoothly to `rgba(201, 164, 92, 0.6)`.

### Section Eyebrows & Dividers
- **Eyebrow Composition:** Upper-case gold tracking accompanied by a single centered hairline diamond or subtle rule flourish (`height: 1px`, gradient from transparent to `#C9A45C` to transparent).

### USP Icon Tiles
- **Form:** Elevated square tiles with `1.25rem` corner radius, `#FFFFFF` fill, and a `1px` subtle gold rim.
- **Icon Styling:** Refined line-weight vectors (stroke `1.5px`) in deep `#006D56`, resting inside a soft ivory circular well (`#FCF9F2`).

### Sticky Navigation Header
- **Structure:** Slim height (`72px`), pinned atop viewport. Backdrop filter with `rgba(252, 249, 242, 0.85)` ivory diffusion, separating from page content via a single `rgba(201, 164, 92, 0.2)` baseline. Houses the serif brand crest, clean category links, and a compact "Book Appointment" pill button.

### Form Inputs & Checkboxes
- **Input Fields:** Soft rounded wells (`0.75rem`), background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E8E2D2`. Focused state transitions border to `#006D56` with an ethereal ring of `rgba(0, 109, 86, 0.12)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Circular radio toggles and soft-corner checkboxes with custom emerald `#006D56` fills and white check indicators.
