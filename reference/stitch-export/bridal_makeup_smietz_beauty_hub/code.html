Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about preparing for your celebratory moments.

How early should I begin my pre-bridal treatments? expand_more

We recommend scheduling your first consultation 2 to 3 months prior to your primary ceremony. This timeline allows us to sequence restorative clinical facials, skin de-pigmentation, and hair nourishment rituals safely without overwhelming your skin barrier.

Do you provide a bridal makeup and hair trial? expand_more

Yes. A dedicated trial appointment allows us to test base foundations under different lighting conditions, finalize dupatta pinning techniques, and map exact flower placements so wedding morning runs seamlessly.

Can pre-bridal packages be customized for specific skin needs? expand_more

Absolutely. We never employ cookie-cutter regimens. Following an initial skin and scalp diagnosis, your regimen is customized to target your specific concerns whether it is sensitivity, active acne, dryness, or tan removal.

How do I book dates for my wedding and reception? expand_more

Because we host an intimate number of brides to ensure total focus and calm, wedding calendar slots fill months in advance. Simply message our bridal coordinator directly via WhatsApp with your ceremony dates and event timings.
