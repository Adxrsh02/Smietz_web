Frequently Asked Questions

How do I choose the right service? expand_more

We always encourage first-time visitors to book our complimentary skin or scalp analysis. Our founder Sushama and senior practitioners will evaluate your hair density, moisture balance, or skin barrier to recommend the most restorative treatment ritual.

How can I inquire about service costs? expand_more

Click 'Enquire on WhatsApp' or chat with us directly for transparent pricing based on hair length, density, and skin condition. Because our treatments use customized formulations, we provide an exact quote upon knowing your hair or skin requirements.

Do I need an appointment in advance? expand_more

Yes, advance booking is highly recommended to secure your preferred senior stylist or clinical aesthetician. Services like the Fresh Fruit Facial and Bridal Trousseau trials require curated fresh preparation and dedicated time slots.
