Since opening our doors in 1989 in Mulund West, Smietz Beauty Hub has been dedicated to celebrating and enhancing individual beauty. Under the mindful guidance of Sushama, our sanctuary has nurtured generations of women through thoughtful aesthetic care and artisanal precision.

Rather than adopting generic solutions, our practice centers on thorough consultations, understanding your skin's unique rhythm, and applying refined techniques that produce radiant, enduring results. Every treatment is a bespoke ritual crafted specifically for you.
